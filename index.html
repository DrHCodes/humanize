About Humanize

Humanize was born out of my late-night musings as a sleep-deprived, creatively underutilized, intellectually overstimulated engineer in grad school. The question: What if someone could obsess over human centricity across technologies—versatile enough in tech, UX, and market strategy to revolutionize B2C solutions, technical enough to handle frontier tech and deep tech spaces?

Not endless design thinking workshops. Not industrial design without performance. Systems design for markets disrupted daily by automation and integration. I blend technological depth with user-centric design in ways most disciplines never touch. The secret? Curiosity.

I became an engineer out of pure love for analytic thinking, got addicted to entrepreneurship through a Stanford program in 2014, and shaped my whole career around it—becoming a multi-microspecialist who can handle everything from CAD design and app interfaces to user testing and business model evaluations. My track record: PhD from USC focused on design and creativity, senior engineer at Medtronic, user research at a venture studio, and product management certifications along the way.

Humanize's first project tackled obstetric injuries. Since then: insulin pumps, productivity apps, music tech, grief support, and more. I humanize even the toughest technologies across hardware, software, ML, and services.

Let's make your technologies human-centric and your vision technologically bulletproof.

Research & Insights

Rigorous research methodologies that inform product decisions—from PhD-level academic work to rapid usability testing.

User research isn't one-size-fits-all. The right methodology depends on your product stage, timeline, and questions you need answered.

Generative / Discovery Research

Methods: User Interviews, Field Studies / Ethnography, Contextual Inquiry, Diary Studies, Surveys, Focus Groups
Output: Research Insights, User Journeys, Personas

Evaluative Methods

Methods: Usability Testing, A/B Tests, Card Sorting, Tree Tests, Analytics, Heatmaps
Output: Go/No-Go Decisions, Product Recommendations, Performance Insights

Strategic Research

Methods: Literature Reviews, Competitive Analysis
Output: Feature Prioritization, Feasibility Decisions
